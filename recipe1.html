Pizza

Preparation Time: 40 minutes

Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients:

  • Pizza base
  • Tomato sauce
  • Cheese
  • Onion
  • Capsicum
  • Olives
  • Oregano

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Spread tomato sauce on the pizza base.
  3. Add chopped vegetables.
  4. Sprinkle cheese on top.
  5. Bake for 15-20 minutes.
  6. Take out, slice, and serve hot.

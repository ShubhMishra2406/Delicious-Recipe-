Burger

Preparation Time: 25 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients:

  • Burger buns
  • Vegetable patty
  • Cheese slice
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato slices
  • Onion rings
  • Ketchup
  • Mayonnaise

Steps:

  1. Toast the burger buns lightly.
  2. Cook the vegetable patty.
  3. Spread ketchup and mayonnaise on buns.
  4. Place lettuce, patty, and cheese on the bun.
  5. Add tomato and onion slices.
  6. Cover with the top bun and serve.

Caesar Salad

Preparation Time: 15 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients:

  • Lettuce leaves
  • Croutons
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Caesar dressing
  • Olive oil
  • Black pepper

Steps:

  1. Wash and chop the lettuce leaves.
  2. Add croutons to the bowl.
  3. Pour Caesar dressing and mix well.
  4. Sprinkle parmesan cheese on top.
  5. Add olive oil and black pepper.
  6. Toss everything and serve fresh.

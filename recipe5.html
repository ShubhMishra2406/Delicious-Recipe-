Samosa

Preparation Time: 45 minutes

Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients:

  • All-purpose flour (maida)
  • Boiled potatoes
  • Peas
  • Green chilies
  • Garam masala
  • Salt
  • Oil (for frying)

Steps:

  1. Prepare dough using flour, salt, and water.
  2. Mash boiled potatoes and mix with peas and spices.
  3. Roll the dough and cut into halves.
  4. Shape into cones and fill with potato mixture.
  5. Seal the edges properly.
  6. Deep fry until golden brown.
  7. Serve hot with chutney.
